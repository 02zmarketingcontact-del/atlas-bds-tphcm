# Industrial discovery pilot

The KCN tab is for **location research**, not live industrial sale/rental inventory.

InlandV (https://inlandv.com/) is a useful reference for distinct user needs:
- industrial land *inside* an industrial park;
- land *outside* industrial parks;
- ready-built factories;
- warehouses and logistics.

Do not reuse copyrighted text, images or scrape listings without permission. The initial six industrial locations are research candidates and require project-level document checks, coordinates, available areas, prices and utility suitability before publication as commercial stock. They are deliberately not inserted into the apartment price map.

The planning map's community boundary data (Free-GIS-Data) is not certified parcel-level planning. Metro line 1 is displayed as approximate visual geometry, not surveyed alignment. Overlay errors must never be misrepresented as official planning confirmation.
